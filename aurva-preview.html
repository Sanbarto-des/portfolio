01

context.

What is Aurva?

Aurva is a data-security company that brings real-time visibility and control over how data is accessed and used, across humans, services and AI agents. Moving beyond static security models, it helps businesses detect, understand and prevent unauthorized access instantly.

The need

Aurva's early visual presence reflected a functional, product-first approach. Clean but generic. The interface lacked a distinct identity, making it difficult for the brand to stand out in an increasingly competitive B2B landscape. As the category evolved, leading players like Cyera, Wiz & Upwind began establishing strong, recognizable visual systems that blended product clarity with a unique brand voice. Aurva lacked this consistency and memorability.

My Task

To design and establish a consistent, scalable brand identity, one that not only supports the product but elevates Aurva into a brand people recognize and remember.

strategy.

Brand StrategyCore values

Aurva is built on the principles of clarity, trust and intelligent design, simplifying complex systems into transparent, reliable experiences, while maintaining consistency across every interaction.

Clarity

Complex systems made transparent and easy to read.

Trust

Security you can rely on, communicated with confidence.

Intelligence

Active, adaptive systems that understand context.

Consistency

One coherent language across every surface.

Brand personality

Aurva's personality is defined through a set of carefully chosen attributes, a collective expression of how the brand thinks, behaves and communicates.

Branding ConceptCompetitor observation

An analysis of competitor brands revealed a strong emphasis on distinct visual identities and cohesive systems. This highlighted the need for Aurva to move beyond functional design and establish a more recognizable, differentiated presence, e.g. a mascot, eye-catching branding visuals and a repeatable system.

Defining the vision

Early conversations with the team pointed to a single, resonant direction: a space-inspired universe, spacecraft and planets for nodes and connections, a subtle character for approachability, and a sense of scale and system.

Protected Intelligence

The mascot is built around the idea of a valuable pearl protected within a shell, a direct reflection of Aurva's core philosophy. The pearl represents sensitive and valuable data: intelligent, essential and worth protecting. The surrounding shell symbolizes Aurva's role as a secure, protective layer.

04

system.

StylesheetThe logo

Aurva already had its logo and primary visual direction established before the project began. My responsibility was to refine and standardize the brand system, defining clear guidelines for spacing, sizing, alignment and color combinations to ensure consistency across all visual touchpoints.

Color system

The palette reflects Aurva's identity as a modern, enterprise-focused data-security company. The primary green represents trust, security and active system intelligence; the secondary purple introduces depth, innovation and a futuristic, technological feel. A balanced neutral grey scale keeps clarity, hierarchy and professionalism across interfaces.

Visual ElementsA space-themed metaphor system

Because the brand world is set in outer space, each recurring element maps to a concept in data infrastructure, turning an abstract security story into an intuitive, memorable visual language.

Space Jewels

Valuable data assets, the things worth protecting. Rendered in green and purple crystal facets.

Asteroid Stones

Risks and potential threats, risky identities and hazards drifting through the system.

Planets

Databases, large, orbiting bodies that hold and organize information at scale.

Spaceships

Accessors and moving entities, the humans, services and agents traversing the system.

Space Land

An illustrated spacescape that sets the scene, the environment the whole system lives in.

AI Sidekick

A side-kick character that suggests an AI agent, an approachable companion working alongside the user.

Key learnings & takeaways

Working on Aurva gave me valuable experience developing core branding concepts supported by meaningful visual assets. It taught me how to translate an abstract, enterprise data-security story into a warm, memorable universe, and how to execute a unified brand language across a wide range of deliverables while holding consistency, clarity and visual impact throughout.
