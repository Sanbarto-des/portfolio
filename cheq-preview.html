What is CheQ?

CheQ is a Bengaluru-based fintech platform focused on helping users manage their credit more effectively. It lets users pay credit-card bills, track their credit health, and earn rewards through a unified ecosystem. Positioned as a credit-management platform rather than just a payment app, CheQ aims to simplify and improve how individuals interact with credit by offering insights, reminders, and reward-driven experiences.

Why illustrations matter in fintech

In fintech, concepts like credit, rewards, and financial transactions can often feel abstract and complex to users. Illustrations help simplify these ideas by turning them into visual stories that are easier to understand and relate to. They also add a sense of approachability and trust, making the experience feel less intimidating. Beyond clarity, illustrations play a key role in creating a strong brand identity and enhancing user engagement across both product and marketing touchpoints.

My role

I worked with CheQ as an illustrator for 7 months, where I focused on building a scalable illustration library based on a newly defined design style. Using provided guidelines and reference visuals, I developed a cohesive system of illustrations and reusable components to ensure consistency across product and marketing.

Illustration development process

The illustration process followed a structured three-step approach: from a loose exploration of form to a clean, colored vector output built on the predefined color library.

Step 1
Rough Sketch

Develop the composition through rough hand-drawn sketches to explore layout and form.

Step 2
Line Art

Define the selected sketch by tracing it digitally to create clean, precise vector line art.

Step 3
Fair Illustration

Apply vector fills and colors based on the predefined color library to achieve the final output.

Visual styleColor usage

Bright · positive states

The use of bright colors in positive-state illustrations helps create a sense of reward, celebration, and achievement. These vibrant visuals instantly draw attention and reinforce positive user actions, making interactions feel more engaging and satisfying. By associating success states with high-contrast, energetic palettes, the experience becomes more intuitive, uplifting, and emotionally rewarding.

Neutral grey · negative states

A neutral grey palette for negative or error states creates a sense of subtlety and restraint without overwhelming the user. Instead of harsh or alarming colors, muted tones communicate issues in a calm, non-intrusive way, reducing anxiety. This keeps visual balance while still clearly indicating a problem: controlled, thoughtful, and user-friendly.

Depth & perspective

The illustration style incorporates subtle depth and perspective to create a more engaging, dynamic experience. Instead of relying on flat 2D views, elements are designed with slight angles to add a sense of dimension and realism.

The same perspective grid and light direction carry across every object, cards, coins, documents and folders, so the whole set stays cohesive even as the subject matter changes.

Motion-readiness

The system is designed with motion readiness in mind, allowing elements to be easily translated into animations when required. Individual components such as chips, cards, and tokens are built as modular units, enabling smooth transitions, rotations, and interactions.

ComponentsThe building blocks

CheQ Chip

Chips serve as the core visual element of the system, representing rewards and transactions. Designed with consistent shape, color, and depth, they act as versatile building blocks across multiple use cases.

Credit Cards

Card components are created in multiple variations to reflect different states and contexts within the product. Their consistent perspective and structure ensure clarity while adapting to diverse scenarios.

Micro Elements

Micro-elements such as confetti, sparkles and motion lines add layers of expression to the system. They enhance moments of feedback, celebration, and interaction, making the experience more engaging.

Customizable Human Assets

Human characters were designed as modular, customizable figures, with variations in hairstyles, facial hair, accessories, and outfits, keeping them visually diverse and adaptable while maintaining overall consistency.
